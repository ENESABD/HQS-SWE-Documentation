**What We Build With**

Technologies are tools that solve developer problems. Frameworks, languages, and libraries exist because someone identified a recurring challenge and built a solution. Choosing the right technology means understanding what problem it solves.

## Topics Covered

- **High-Level Languages & Frameworks** - Abstraction that enables productivity
- **Why Frameworks Exist** - Solving developer problems
- **ORMs & Abstraction Layers** - Simplifying data access
- **Choosing the Right Tool** - Matching technology to problem
