**People First**

Software is built by people, for people. Before we discuss how to write code or what tools to use, we must understand how to work together effectively. The human element is often the determining factor in project success.

## Topics Covered

- **Team Dynamics & Communication** - How effective teams collaborate
- **Planning & Estimation** - Realistic commitments, iterative planning
- **Managing Pressure & Accountability** - Sustainable pace, handling stakeholders
- **Culture** - Trust, psychological safety, continuous improvement
