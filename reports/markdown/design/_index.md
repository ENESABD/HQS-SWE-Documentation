**How We Think About Code**

Good design emerges from working code through continuous refactoring. These principles and patterns help us recognize opportunities for improvement and guide our refactoring decisions.

## Topics Covered

- **Object-Oriented Principles (APIEC)** - Abstraction, Polymorphism, Inheritance, Encapsulation, Composition
- **SOLID Principles** - Guidelines for maintainable object-oriented design
- **Design Patterns** - Proven solutions to recurring problems
- **Refactoring** - Improving code structure without changing behavior
