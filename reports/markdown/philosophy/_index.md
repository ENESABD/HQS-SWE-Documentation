**What Software Engineering Really Is**

Software engineering is the discipline of solving problems through the design, construction, and maintenance of software systems — managing the unique complexities that arise from software's abstract, malleable, and interconnected nature.

## Topics Covered

- **What is Software Engineering** - Problem solving through software as a medium
- **Defining Success** - Value delivered, not schedules met
- **The Agile Mindset** - Embracing change, delivering continuously
- **Why Quality Matters** - Building software that lasts
